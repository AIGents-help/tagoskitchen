# Commercial Kitchen Renter Use, Risk Allocation, and Indemnity Agreement

STATUS: ATTORNEY-REVIEW DRAFT — NOT ACTIVE

This Agreement is between [TAGO'S LEGAL ENTITY] (Kitchen Owner), [PLATFORM LEGAL ENTITY] (Platform), and the renter identified in the booking (Renter). It applies to each reservation, all persons Renter brings onto the premises, and all food or products Renter prepares, stores, transports, sells, or serves.

## 1. Independent business
Renter operates an independent business and is not an employee, partner, joint venturer, franchisee, or agent of Kitchen Owner or Platform. Renter has no authority to bind either party and is solely responsible for its workers, contractors, customers, taxes, payroll, permits, and business operations.

## 2. Renter's primary responsibility
To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Renter knowingly assumes full responsibility for Renter's activities, personnel, ingredients, food, products, customers, equipment use, regulatory compliance, sanitation, cleanup, storage, transport, sales, and consequences arising from them. Neither Kitchen Owner nor Platform supervises Renter's food production or guarantees that Renter's practices, recipes, labels, products, or business comply with law.

## 3. Licenses and credentials
Renter represents continuously that every required food-handler certificate, business license, permit, commissary document, insurance policy, vehicle approval, and other credential is genuine, current, accurate, and sufficient for Renter's activities. Platform verification is administrative only, may be incomplete, and does not transfer responsibility or constitute legal, safety, or regulatory approval.

## 4. Food safety and illness
Renter is solely responsible for approved sourcing, receiving, time and temperature control, cross-contamination prevention, employee health exclusions, handwashing, cooking, cooling, reheating, holding, labeling, traceability, recall cooperation, and disposal. Renter must immediately report suspected contamination, illness, adulteration, or recall and stop affected operations.

## 5. Allergens and special diets
Renter assumes sole responsibility for ingredient disclosures, allergen controls, separation, cleaning, labeling, customer communications, and all allergy or dietary claims. The kitchen is shared and is not represented as allergen-free, gluten-free, kosher, halal, or dedicated unless separately confirmed in writing.

## 6. Personnel and guests
Renter is responsible for every employee, contractor, helper, driver, customer, child, guest, or invitee Renter brings to the property. Only approved individuals may enter production areas. Renter must provide required training and supervision and prevent harassment, intoxication, unsafe conduct, and unauthorized access.

## 7. Equipment
Renter may use only equipment included in the reservation and only if trained and competent. Renter must inspect equipment before use, report defects immediately, follow manufacturer and posted instructions, use required protective equipment, and stop using unsafe equipment. Renter is responsible for misuse, loss, extraordinary cleaning, and damage caused by Renter or Renter's invitees, ordinary wear excepted.

## 8. Sanitation and closeout
Renter must complete opening and closing inspections, maintain sanitation during use, prevent pests, remove food and waste, clean and sanitize assigned areas and equipment, return items to designated locations, and complete the digital closeout checklist. Kitchen Owner may charge documented cleaning, disposal, repair, replacement, overtime, pest-control, or emergency-response costs attributable to Renter.

## 9. Shared-space risks
Renter understands that other independent businesses may use the property. Renter must remain within reserved zones and times, protect property and confidential information, avoid interference, and report conflicts. Renter accepts ordinary shared-facility risks, including residual allergens and scheduling interruptions, subject to duties that cannot legally be waived.

## 10. Insurance
Before access, Renter must maintain all legally required insurance and the coverage specified for the kitchen, including commercial general and product liability coverage. When required, Kitchen Owner and Platform must be named as additional insureds on a primary and noncontributory basis, with waiver of subrogation where commercially available. Renter must notify Platform before cancellation or material reduction.

## 11. Customers and sales
Renter is merchant of record for its own food and services unless a written payment schedule states otherwise. Renter is solely responsible for product descriptions, pricing, fulfillment, refunds, taxes, chargebacks, consumer complaints, privacy notices, and customer claims. Payment processing does not make Kitchen Owner or Platform the seller, manufacturer, distributor, or guarantor of Renter's products.

## 12. Assumption of risk
Renter understands commercial kitchens involve heat, flame, hot oil, steam, sharp tools, heavy equipment, wet floors, chemicals, lifting, electrical and gas appliances, foodborne illness, and other known and unknown hazards. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Renter voluntarily assumes risks arising from Renter's occupancy, conduct, personnel, production, and use of equipment.

## 13. Release
To the fullest extent permitted by law, Renter releases Kitchen Owner, Platform, their affiliates, property owners, and their officers, employees, and agents from claims arising from Renter's activities or assumed risks. This release does not apply where prohibited by law and does not waive liability that cannot lawfully be waived, including liability that a court determines resulted from a released party's gross negligence, reckless conduct, willful misconduct, or other non-waivable duty.

## 14. Indemnification and defense
To the fullest extent permitted by law, Renter will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Kitchen Owner, Platform, the property owner, and their affiliates, officers, employees, and agents from third-party claims, losses, penalties, recalls, chargebacks, damages, judgments, and reasonable legal costs arising from Renter's food, products, personnel, customers, regulatory violations, breach, negligence, misconduct, or use of the property, except to the extent finally determined to result from an indemnified party's non-waivable wrongdoing.

## 15. Security deposit and payment authorization
Renter authorizes charges for reservations, approved extensions, documented damage, missing property, extraordinary cleaning, disposal, access violations, chargebacks, and other amounts expressly permitted by the booking terms. Any deposit is not a limit on Renter's responsibility. Charges may be disputed through the stated process.

## 16. Incidents, evidence, and cooperation
Renter must immediately protect life and safety, contact emergency services when appropriate, notify Kitchen Owner, preserve relevant food and records, complete an incident report, and cooperate with insurers and lawful investigations. Renter may not conceal an incident or make unauthorized admissions on behalf of Kitchen Owner or Platform.

## 17. Suspension and termination
Access may be denied or suspended for expired credentials, nonpayment, unsafe conduct, contamination, material breach, suspected fraud, regulatory direction, emergency, or risk to people, property, licensing, or reputation. Emergency suspension does not require advance notice.

## 18. Law, disputes, and enforceability
Governing law, venue, arbitration or court process, jury waiver, class-action waiver, notice addresses, limitation periods, damages limitations, and any prevailing-party provision must be completed and approved by licensed counsel for the kitchen's jurisdiction. If a provision is unenforceable, it will be narrowed only to the extent legally permitted without invalidating the remainder.

## Electronic acceptance
By signing electronically, Renter confirms that Renter read the entire Agreement, had an opportunity to seek legal advice, understands that material rights are affected, accepts responsibility for Renter's business and invitees, and has authority to bind the identified business. Acceptance is recorded with the exact agreement version and document hash.
